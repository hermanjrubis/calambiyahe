About

Calzada is the word for road or pathway. It's also a commuter guide for getting around Calamba, built around direction, clarity, and movement.

Calzada helps you discover places worth visiting around Calamba, then shows you how to get there. Compare travel options based on estimated duration, fare cost, and walking distance — by jeepney or on foot — to find the most convenient way to reach your destination.

The platform is fully responsive on desktop and mobile, with a simple interface that makes route planning easy even for first-time users.

Goal

Calzada helps commuters find places worth visiting in Calamba and get there by jeepney or on foot, with clear directions, fares and travel times. By bringing places, routes and fares together, it aims to make getting around the city less stressful and help you plan your time and budget.

What Calzada covers

Currently covering 5 barangays in Calamba: Uno, Real, Halang, Lecheria and Bucal. More barangays will be added.
